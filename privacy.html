Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026 · Applies to the GHAR DEKHO website and Android app
In short
  • We collect only what's needed to post and find properties, chat and schedule visits.
  • Listings are public: anyone can see a listing's details, including the owner's name, photo and phone number.
  • No ads, no analytics trackers, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and data any time from Edit Profile → Delete Account.

1. Who we are

GHAR DEKHO ("we", "us") is a platform that connects property owners with people looking for rooms, homes, plots, shops and study spaces in India. We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact and grievances: ghardekho.help@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address, and whether you are an owner or a seeker. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it.
  • Profile (optional): phone number, profile photo, a short bio, and your city.
  • Listings you post: title, price, deposit, address, area and PIN code, photos, amenities, required documents (a list of document names only; we never ask you to upload documents), and the owner name, photo and phone number shown on the listing.
  • Messages you send to other users through in-app chat, including whether a message has been seen.
  • Visit requests: the date, time and message you send, and their status.
  • Saved properties, notifications, users you block, and reports you file about listings or users.

Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location (only if you allow it): your device's location is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find your city name. We save only the city name to your profile, never your exact coordinates. You can deny location access and type your city instead.
  • Stored on your device only: your language choice and your "Recently Viewed" list are kept in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.
  • Technical data: our hosting and database providers may automatically log IP addresses, browser/device type and request times to operate and secure the service.

What we do not collect

We do not collect payment details, your contacts list, government ID numbers or documents, or precise GPS location. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show your listings to people searching, and to show you listings near your city.
  • To deliver messages, visit requests and in-app notifications between users.
  • To review reports, remove fake or abusive listings, and suspend accounts that break our Terms & Conditions (in the app menu).
  • To reply when you contact us.

We rely on your consent (given when you create an account and post content) and on our legitimate need to keep the platform safe.

4. Who can see your data

⚠️ Public: everything in a listing, including the owner's name, profile photo and phone number, can be seen by anyone who opens the app or the listing link, even without logging in. Don't put anything in a listing you don't want public.
  • Other users: only the people you chat with or request visits from see those messages and requests, plus the name you used.
  • Our admins: can view account details (name, email, phone, city), listings and reports to moderate the platform. Reports are visible only to admins; the person you report is never told who reported them.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We will disclose data only if required by Indian law or a valid legal order.

5. Where it's stored and how it's protected

Data is stored by the providers above and may be kept on servers outside India. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and database access rules ensure that, for example, only chat participants can read a chat and only you can read your saved items, notifications and blocked list. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share passwords or send advance payments to anyone you haven't verified.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete it any time:

  • In the app: open the menu → Edit Profile → Delete Account.
  • From the web: use the button below, log in, and confirm.
  • By email: write to ghardekho.help@gmail.com from your account's email address.
Delete my account

Deleted immediately: your login, profile (name, phone, photo, bio, city), all your listings, saved properties, notifications and blocked list. Open visit requests are cancelled.

What may remain:

  • Messages you already sent stay visible to the person you sent them to, and past visit requests stay in the other person's history, because they are part of that person's records too.
  • Reports you filed are kept for safety review.
  • Uploaded photo files may remain in our image storage after the listing is removed. They are no longer shown in the app. Email us to have them permanently erased.
  • If an account was suspended for breaking our rules, a minimal record is kept to prevent abuse, but personal details are removed.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it's used,
  • correct or update it (you can also edit your profile and listings yourself),
  • erase it (see section 6),
  • withdraw your consent, by deleting your account,
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity, and
  • address a grievance.

Email ghardekho.help@gmail.com. We will respond as soon as we can, and within the time required by law. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

GHAR DEKHO is meant for adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll change the effective date above, and for significant changes we'll show a notice in the app.
